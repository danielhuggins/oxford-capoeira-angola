About

Angoleiros do Mar

Oxford Capoeira Society is a project developed in Oxford through Angoleiros do Mar, an international Capoeira Angola group led by Mestre Marcelo Angola. Rooted in the Capoeira Angola tradition of Bahia, Angoleiros do Mar has worked for more than 20 years to keep this practice alive in Oxford, sharing it with students at Oxford University and Oxford Brookes University, as well as with the wider local community. The group welcomes students, professionals, local residents, and anyone curious to learn.

Teacher

Professor Lobinho

Mauricio Jiménez Mota has 16 years of experience in Capoeira Angola under the guidance of Mestre Marcelo Angola, leader of Angoleiros do Mar, originally from Ilha de Itaparica, Bahia.

Schools

Workshops for schools

Schools can contact Oxford Capoeira Society to arrange Capoeira Angola workshops, cultural presentations, or movement and music sessions for students.

Sessions introduce students to Capoeira Angola through movement, rhythm, singing, instruments, history, and group participation.

First class

Try your first class

Wear comfortable clothes such as sports trousers, a T-shirt, and shoes you can move in. Bring a bottle of water.

All levels are accepted, from beginners to experienced students. You do not need any previous capoeira experience.
