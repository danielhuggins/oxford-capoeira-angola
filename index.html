About

Rooted in Capoeira Angola tradition

Capoeira Angola brings together movement, music, rhythm, singing, community, and cultural tradition. The group welcomes students, professionals, local residents, and anyone curious to learn.

First class

Try your first class

Wear comfortable clothes such as sports trousers, a comfortable T-shirt, and shoes you can move in. Bring a bottle of water.

All levels are accepted, from beginners to experienced students. You do not need any previous capoeira experience.
