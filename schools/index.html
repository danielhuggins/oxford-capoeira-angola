Capoeira workshops for schools in Oxford

Oxford Capoeira Society offers Capoeira Angola workshops for schools, introducing students to Afro-Brazilian culture through movement, music, rhythm, instruments, history, and group participation.

Email about a school workshop

What a workshop can include

Movement

Students explore adaptable Capoeira Angola movements in a safe and inclusive way.

Music and rhythm

Workshops can introduce clapping, singing, berimbau, percussion, and call-and-response.

Culture and history

Sessions can introduce Capoeira Angola as a living Afro-Brazilian cultural tradition.

Participation

Activities are adaptable for different ages, spaces, timetables, and group sizes.

Oxford schools

Education, culture, and community

School workshops can support cultural learning, physical education, music, confidence, coordination, listening, and shared participation. We can discuss the right format for your class, year group, or school event.
