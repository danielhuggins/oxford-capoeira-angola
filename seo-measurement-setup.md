# SEO Measurement Setup

Use this checklist to measure whether SEO changes are helping Oxford Capoeira Society.

## First Priority: Google Search Console

Google Search Console is the most useful first step because it shows how people find the site in Google.

### Setup Steps

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. Add property: `https://www.oxfordcapoeira.co.uk/`
3. Choose a verification method.
4. If possible, use DNS verification through the domain provider.
5. Submit sitemap: `https://www.oxfordcapoeira.co.uk/sitemap.xml`

### What To Check Monthly

- Search queries bringing impressions.
- Clicks for terms such as `capoeira oxford`, `capoeira classes oxford`, `capoeira angola oxford`.
- Pages receiving impressions.
- Indexing status for `/`, `/classes/`, `/schools/`, `/events/`, and `/community-impact/`.

## Second Priority: Privacy-Friendly Analytics

Recommended options:

- Cloudflare Web Analytics: free and lightweight.
- Plausible: simple and privacy-friendly, paid.
- Google Analytics: powerful, but heavier and more complex.

Do not add analytics code until the account is created and the exact tracking snippet is available.

## Useful Conversion Events

Track these manually at first:

- WhatsApp clicks.
- Email clicks.
- Instagram clicks.
- Visits to `/classes/`.
- Visits to `/schools/`.
- Visits to `/community-impact/`.

## Monthly Review Questions

- Which search terms are growing?
- Are people finding adult classes, children's classes, or school workshops?
- Which page leads to the most WhatsApp or email enquiries?
- Are visitors coming from Oxford and nearby areas?
