Children's Capoeira Angola classes in Oxford

Oxford Capoeira Society offers a children's Capoeira Angola class for ages 5 to 16 in Oxford. The class introduces movement, rhythm, music, confidence, coordination, and cultural learning in a friendly group environment.

For parents

Movement, music, and culture

Children learn Capoeira Angola through adaptable movement, rhythm, listening, singing, instruments, and group participation. The class supports confidence, coordination, focus, creativity, and respect for others.

No previous capoeira, martial arts, dance, or music experience is needed. Message us before coming so we can welcome your child and answer any questions.
