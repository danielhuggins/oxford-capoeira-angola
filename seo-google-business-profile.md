## Short Description

Oxford Capoeira Society offers beginner-friendly Capoeira Angola classes in Oxford for adults and children, combining movement, music, rhythm, culture, and community.

## Longer Description

Oxford Capoeira Society shares Capoeira Angola in Oxford through regular adult classes, children's classes, rodas, workshops, music, movement, and cultural education. The group is connected to Angoleiros do Mar, led by Mestre Marcelo Angola, and welcomes beginners with no previous capoeira, martial arts, dance, or music experience.

Classes take place in Oxford venues including Jericho Community Centre, West Oxford Community Association, and Brookes University Sports. Message us before your first class so we can suggest the best session for you.
